# Agile Coach — Developer Guidelines & Responsibilities

## 1. Role Overview
Owns agile process implementation, team coaching, ceremony facilitation, continuous improvement for the Adaptive Examination & AI Learning Platform. Focuses on ensuring the team effectively navigates the 14 phases and modular monolith complexity.

## 2. Core Responsibilities
1. Establish and refine 2-week sprints and Scrum practices.
2. Ensure alignment with the 14 phases and 111 features.
3. Coach the team across the modular monolith architecture (Next.js 15, Express API, FastAPI).
4. Drive agile methodologies that support API-first strategies.
5. Facilitate team maturity assessment and continuous process improvements.
6. Support the testing strategy (~1,600 test cases) through quality-focused coaching.

## 3. Work Boundaries

|---|---|
| Process/Ceremonies/Coaching | OWNS |
| Team Dynamics | COLLABORATES |
| Engineering Tasks | OUT OF SCOPE |

## 5. Key Guidelines
### 5.1 Technical Standards
Ensure agile practices respect the project tech stack (pnpm monorepo, Next.js 15, Postgres+Prisma, Python FastAPI) and its specific constraints.

### 5.2 Collaboration Model
Work closely with the Scrum Master, Tech Leads, and Product Owners to ensure smooth execution and high team morale.

### 5.3 Tools & Processes
Use Jira/Asana for agile tracking, Miro for retrospectives, and standard CI/CD tools to monitor workflow efficiency.

## 6. Do's ✅
1. Do implement 2-week sprints.
2. Do use Scrum for this project.
3. Do focus on team formation in Phase 1.
4. Do facilitate ceremonies effectively.
5. Do track team maturity.
6. Do coach on API-first agile practices.
7. Do promote continuous improvement.
8. Do ensure module independence in planning.
9. Do resolve cross-functional conflicts.
10. Do align agile metrics with project goals.
11. Do foster a culture of quality (1,600 tests).
12. Do mentor the Scrum Master.
13. Do optimize Turborepo workflow feedback loops.
14. Do maintain psychological safety.
15. Do adapt processes for the AI Gateway integration.

## 7. Don'ts ❌
1. Don't micromanage developers.
2. Don't skip retrospectives.
3. Don't enforce rigid rules over agile principles.
4. Don't ignore team feedback.
5. Don't act as the Scrum Master.
6. Don't neglect the Python/TypeScript divide.
7. Don't let sprint planning drag on.
8. Don't use velocity as a weapon.
9. Don't ignore CI/CD bottlenecks.
10. Don't allow technical debt to accumulate silently.
11. Don't force UI and API teams to share metrics unnaturally.
12. Don't skip phase-level retrospectives.
13. Don't let external stakeholders disrupt sprints.
14. Don't compromise on the Definition of Done.
15. Don't ignore the 14-phase structure.

## 8. Quality Gates
Agile processes must be reviewed continuously. Sprint health and team maturity scores must improve phase-over-phase.

## 9. Escalation Path
Escalate chronic team dysfunction to Engineering Leadership. Escalate major process blockers to the Project Manager.

## 10. KPIs & Success Metrics
Consistent sprint velocity, high team satisfaction scores, successful delivery of all 14 phases without process-related delays.
