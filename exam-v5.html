⚠ UI REFERENCE v5 — Ctrl/Cmd + Scroll zooms, plain scroll just scrolls

Hover over either pane and use your mouse wheel with no modifier held — that's plain native scrolling, completely untouched, exactly like before. Hold Ctrl (or Cmd on Mac) while scrolling over a pane, and the same wheel now zooms that pane instead — wheel up/pinch-out zooms in, wheel down/pinch-in zooms out. Nothing needs to be "entered" or toggled between the two modes; the modifier key is the only thing that decides which one happens, so your hand never has to leave the mouse. The +/−/Reset buttons are still there too, doing the exact same thing through a different input.
